Privacy

Last reviewed: 27 August 2026

SurveyCam is designed for field documentation using photos, videos, location details, timestamps, notes, project folders and PDF proof reports.

Current Google Play data-safety declaration

The current public Google Play listing declares that SurveyCam does not share data with third parties and does not collect data.

On-device records

The Play Store description states that photos, videos, notes and reports stay on your phone unless you choose to share them. The listing also describes SurveyCam as having no tracking, no cloud storage and no advertising analytics.

Location and captured media

SurveyCam uses location and camera-related information to provide features such as GPS watermarks, timestamps, address/coordinate details, photos, videos, project records and proof reports. These features are part of the app's field-documentation purpose.

Sharing

SurveyCam includes local gallery and sharing features. When you choose to share media or reports, the destination you select may have its own privacy practices.

Policy changes

If SurveyCam's data practices or features change, this page and the Google Play data-safety information should be updated accordingly.
