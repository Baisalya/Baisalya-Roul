# NotiVault advertising direction

## Primary campaign idea

**Deleted from the chat. Still safe in your vault.**

NotiVault can preserve a message preview when it arrives as a notification after
setup—even if the source app later reports that message as deleted.

## Supporting ad copy

- Catch selected chat notifications before they disappear.
- Keep deleted-message notification previews searchable on your device.
- Save notification-exposed photos, videos and voice notes in your private vault.
- Works after setup with apps you select. No account and no ad tracking.

## Required condition near every deleted-message claim

> The notification must arrive and be captured before the message is deleted.
> Voice notes, photos and videos are saved only when Android and the source app
> expose an accessible notification copy and that media type is enabled.

## Claims not to use

- “Recover any deleted WhatsApp message.”
- “Recover every deleted voice note, photo or video.”
- “Read deleted chats directly from WhatsApp.”
- “Works on messages sent before NotiVault was installed.”
- “Guaranteed recovery.”

Those statements do not match the app. NotiVault reads Android notifications,
not another app's private chat database.
