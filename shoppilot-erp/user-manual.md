# ShopPilot Quick User Manual

ShopPilot helps shop owners and staff manage daily work from one simple app. The main work area changes automatically for a repair shop, bakery, grocery shop, retail shop, restaurant, tailoring business, or garage.

## 1. Start the app

1. Open ShopPilot.
2. Continue with the Google account that should own or access the business.
3. Choose an existing workspace, restore a backup, or create a new business.
4. Select the correct business type.
5. Enter the shop name and basic details.

## 2. Understand the main navigation

- **Home:** See the business summary and important shortcuts.
- **Customers:** Add customers, search records, and open customer history.
- **Work:** Open the main workflow for the selected business type.
- **More / Business tools:** Open reports, ERP tools, backup, branches, staff controls, activity history, and settings.

On Windows, these areas appear in the left navigation. On Android, use the bottom navigation and the **More** menu.

## 3. Use the correct business workflow

### Repair shop

Create repair jobs, select a customer, record the item or device, update the job status, add charges, and complete delivery.

### Bakery

Manage the product catalogue, create customer orders, record due dates, update order status, and open order details.

### Grocery or retail shop

Manage stock, create sales and invoices, add customers, and review business totals.

### Restaurant

Open tables, manage the menu, create orders, send kitchen work, and settle bills.

### Tailoring business

Create tailoring orders, save measurement profiles, manage materials, update order status, and record delivery.

### Garage

Manage vehicles, job cards, parts, reminders, counter sales, job progress, and garage reports.

## 4. Add a customer and create work

1. Open **Customers**.
2. Search for the customer first to avoid duplicates.
3. Add the customer when no match exists.
4. Open **Work**.
5. Create the job, order, sale, or service record.
6. Check the customer, branch, amount, due date, and status.
7. Tap **Save**, **Continue**, **Create**, or **Checkout**.

ShopPilot saves normal work locally first, so the business can continue when the internet is unavailable.

## 5. Use ERP and business tools

Open **Business tools** to access the features allowed for your role:

- Stock and inventory
- Purchasing
- Staff management
- Reminders
- Backup and recovery
- Data review and conflict handling

Open **Reports** for business summaries, **Accounting** for financial records, and **Activity history** to review important actions.

## 6. Workspaces, branches, and staff

- A **workspace** is a separate business database.
- A **branch** is a location inside that business.
- The **owner** controls business settings, branches, staff access, backup, and recovery.
- A **staff member** sees only the areas allowed by the owner.

Always check the active workspace and branch before saving new records.

## 7. Backup and recovery

1. Open **Settings → Cloud backup → Backup**.
2. Connect the correct Google account.
3. Set up and confirm the recovery code.
4. Export the recovery file and store it somewhere outside the same device.
5. Run the backup and wait for a safe or successful result.

The Google account finds the encrypted backup. The recovery code decrypts it on a replacement device. Without the recovery code, the encrypted backup may not be recoverable.

## 8. Restore on a replacement device

1. Install ShopPilot on the new device.
2. Sign in with the same authorized Google account.
3. Select the discovered workspace.
4. Choose **Restore Data**.
5. Import or enter the matching recovery code.
6. Keep the app open and online during verification and restore.
7. Check customers, recent work, invoices, stock, and branch selection before entering new records.

## 9. Daily safety checklist

Before closing the business for the day, confirm:

- The correct shop and branch were used.
- Jobs and orders have the correct status and due date.
- Payments and invoice totals match the real transactions.
- Backup and team delivery show a safe result.
- The recovery code is stored separately from the device.

Do not uninstall the app, clear app data, or sign out while changes are waiting to be backed up.
