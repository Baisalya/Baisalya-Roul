2. Product purpose

Friction Atlas is a personal habit, behavior-reflection and focus tool. It helps users log habit outcomes, record friction, schedule routines, run focus and walking sessions, test countermeasures and view local pattern/correlation summaries.

It is not medical, psychological, financial, legal or emergency advice and is not a substitute for qualified professional services.

These features should only be enabled if you understand the in-app disclosure and want the related functionality. You can disable optional access through the app and/or operating system settings.

4. Estimates and interpretation

Short-form Reels/Shorts counts, app-open/session counts and correlation outputs are analytical estimates derived from available device events. They may be incomplete or affected by operating-system restrictions and third-party app interface changes.

Correlation insights describe observed associations and must not be interpreted as proof that one behavior caused another.
