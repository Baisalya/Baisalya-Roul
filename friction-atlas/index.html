Capture friction

When a habit is blocked, record the real reason: low energy, no time, scrolling, mood, environment, or your own custom barrier.

⌁

Test countermeasures

Run focused 7-day experiments instead of collecting generic advice. See before-vs-during results and keep what works.

◎

Find correlations

Compare Done vs Missed days with screen time, app opens and observed short-form usage. The app reports associations—not fake causation.

◫

Digital Friction Monitor

Optional Android Usage Access shows per-app foreground time and estimated opens. Enhanced short-form counting is a separate opt-in.

✦

Friction Bot

A draggable companion can surface live context, habit progress and rate-limited reactions without turning the app into a nagging coach.

⌂

Your data stays yours

Core habit history, experiments, correlation evidence and settings are stored locally. Export a backup or delete everything anytime.
