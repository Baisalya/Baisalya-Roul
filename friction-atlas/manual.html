1. Getting started

On a fresh installation, review the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy in the app, tick the agreement checkbox and choose Accept & continue. The bundled English documents work offline. Not now closes the app; accepting does not enable optional tracking or grant device access. Existing onboarded installations retain access without inventing an acceptance record.

Friction Atlas is built around one idea: when a habit does not happen, the miss can become useful evidence. Create a few habits, log them consistently, and add detail only when it helps.

  1. Create your first habit.
  2. Choose the weekdays it should appear.
  3. When it appears Today, mark it Done or Blocked.
  4. If blocked, record the friction and any countermeasure you tried.
  5. After enough history, review Insights and experiments.
The core habit tracker does not require Usage Access, Accessibility, overlay permission or a Friction Atlas account.

2. Habits

Create and schedule

Use clear action names such as “20-minute walk” or “Deep work 45 min” instead of vague goals. Select the weekdays when the habit should be active.

Edit, archive and restore

Habits can be edited, archived, restored or permanently deleted. Archive is useful when you want to preserve history without keeping the habit active.

Daily logging

For each scheduled habit choose Done or Blocked. Undo can restore the preceding daily result after an accidental Done. Today and All habits let you find and edit the right habit. The goal is reliable evidence, not perfect streaks.

3. Blocked days & friction

When a habit is blocked, select or create the reason that best describes what got in the way. Examples include low energy, no time, forgot, too hard, mood, environment and phone/scrolling.

You can record energy, notes and a countermeasure. Later mark whether the countermeasure helped. This is how the app learns which changes repeatedly work for you.

A blocked log is not a failure score. It is an observation used for patterns and experiments.

4. Seven-day experiments

Use experiments when one countermeasure seems worth testing. Friction Atlas compares the period around the experiment so you can see whether the change appears useful.

Only test one meaningful change at a time when possible. Small, specific interventions are easier to evaluate than broad lifestyle promises.

5. Digital Friction Monitor — Android

Digital Friction is optional and split into separate permissions.

Usage Access

With Android Usage Access enabled, the app can summarize local device-derived information such as per-app foreground time, estimated opens/sessions, total app time and top apps.

Enhanced short-form tracking

A separate, optional Accessibility Service can estimate short-form items viewed in supported apps such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and TikTok-style feeds. It waits for identifiable player content to settle and deduplicates repeated items; comments, search and unidentified surfaces are ignored when recognized.

Counts are estimates. Third-party app interfaces change, reverse scrolling can affect events, and Friction Atlas must not present these numbers as exact platform analytics.

The service does not intentionally store messages, passwords, screenshots, images, typed content, raw accessibility trees or screen text.

Turning it off

The Digital Friction master switch is off by default. You can disable it at any time. Delete Everything also removes locally saved digital evidence.

6. App sessions & per-visit counts — Android

Open Digital, then search for an app under Today by app. Tap it to see available visits, each start/end time, foreground duration and observed Reels/Shorts where supported. Show all apps expands the default top-app list.

A visit groups activity/screen transitions within the same app. Switching to another app, locking the screen or a substantial background pause closes the visit. A visit crossing midnight contributes only the time inside today and does not invent an open at midnight. Android’s available foreground events are the source; missing events are not fabricated.

The expanded bot shows the current visit’s time, observed items this visit, that app’s daily total and visit count. Its character-only default stays tiny. Focus, break and walk context take priority while one of those sessions runs.

Per-visit item counts begin with this update. Older visits show a dash instead of an invented zero. A partial label means only part of a visit was covered after recording became available. Daily totals preserve earlier estimates, which cannot be assigned to old visits.

Instagram, YouTube and Facebook can have observed Reels/Shorts counts when the supported player exposes a reliable settled identity. Ordinary time/session counts can be shown for other user-launchable apps. Fast skips and unsupported layouts remain uncounted; these are estimates, not official platform analytics.

Only package, time and item kind are stored for view events, locally. A rolling 30-day cleanup runs when tracking/history is used, with a 50,000-event limit. Reset short-form counter also removes these records. App-session times depend on OS event retention and are not a permanent cloud history.

7. Friction Bot

Android

The optional floating companion requires “Display over other apps.” It can be dragged, snap to an edge, remember position, show supported live context and be paused, hidden or stopped. In Digital, choose Add Quick Settings toggle on Android 13 or later, then confirm the system tile prompt. On older versions, swipe down twice, choose Edit and add Friction Bot. The tile turns the bot on or off; missing setup opens the app without enabling tracking permissions. The running bot notification also has Pause/Resume and Stop controls. In Settings → Your companion → Floating bot size, preview sizes from 100% to 250%. Release the slider to apply; Reset to tiny restores the default. Size is remembered and expanded controls stay compact. Characters keep their proportions. Original artwork is the default; the optional 3D characters use original geometric meshes, lighting and animated poses. Reduce motion freezes their movement. Explore demo in Settings provides sample data, every bot and every expression without an account or purchase. Demo practice does not block apps or read the activity sensor, and never changes your real habits, permissions or purchase ownership. Close it to return to your app.

Windows

The mini companion is an always-on-top app window. It shows local habit progress, experiment context and the selected bot skin. It does not claim Android Usage Access metrics on Windows.

Moods

Companions can use Calm/Idle, Happy, Proud, Curious, Focus, Tired, Concerned and Sleep visual states. The tiny bot has no surrounding card by default. It focuses with you, sleeps when paused and looks concerned after a distraction. Only a reached goal receives its proud achievement reaction; partial progress gets a gentler response. Reactions are rate-limited. Reduced Motion keeps the useful state while limiting motion.

8. Habit correlation

Correlation compares logged Done and Missed days against locally available digital-friction summaries. Metrics can include screen time, app opens, observed short-form counts and top-app time.

Friction Atlas requires minimum evidence before presenting a signal: at least 7 matched days, 3 Done days and 3 Missed days. More history increases the evidence label.

Association is not causation. If Instagram time is higher on missed Deep Work days, the app can show that relationship; it should not claim Instagram caused the miss.

9. Backup, lock & data controls

Habit history, routines, dated occurrences, focus/break/walk sessions and supported local evidence can be exported/imported through the app’s backup tools. Optional device/biometric lock can protect local app access where supported. Restoring an active session from a backup closes it as recovered history rather than restarting a timer, guard or sensor.

Delete Everything clears local app data including correlation evidence. Raw Android OS events and the separate per-view timestamp store are not included in the core JSON backup. Store ownership is different: paid product ownership is handled by the app store and can be restored where supported.

10. Focus sessions & breaks

From Today, choose Start focus. Pick 25 or 50 minutes, or open More options for a custom duration and optional habit link. Name the session if helpful, then Start.

Pause freezes the countdown; Resume continues it. Finish saves your actual elapsed time, including a partial session. Stop without completing records a cancelled session. Completing early never claims a full 25-minute goal.

After focus, choose a separate five- or ten-minute break. Break time does not count as focus time. Session history keeps your records locally. Only one focus, break or walk session runs at a time.

11. Focus guard

  1. Open Focus guard in the Focus setup.
  2. Select the distracting apps you want covered.
  3. On Android, read the disclosure and manually enable Friction Atlas · Focus guard in Accessibility settings.
  4. Leave “Use guard for this session” on and start focus.

Opening a selected app brings up your companion with a reminder, countdown, Back to my task, a one-minute allowance and End focus session. Pause, breaks and session expiry release the guard.

On Windows, open an app before refreshing the app picker and keep Friction Atlas running. The entire selected app is covered, including all tabs of a selected browser. It does not terminate processes. System controls remain available.

Android Focus guard is separate from Enhanced short-form tracking. Its service detects foreground app package changes and does not retrieve screen content. Calls, Home, Settings and permission controls are excluded.

12. Routines, timetable & dated records

Open Routine or use Add → Routine. Give the activity a name, choose its kind, weekdays and time, then set a duration, step target or quantity when needed. Enable a reminder if you want one.

Today materializes the activities due that day. Start a timed activity, record progress, mark a task Done, skip it or snooze its reminder. Routine history retains the day’s title and goal so later schedule changes do not rewrite old records.

Manage your routines to edit them or stop future repeats. An optional habit link can connect completion to a habit; it does not replace an existing explicit habit log. Reliable Windows reminder delivery requires an installed MSIX app.

13. Walking & steps

Choose Walk from Today or start a walking routine. Set a step goal and session duration. Android asks for optional physical activity access to use an available step counter or detector.

Steps belong to the current walk. The first counter reading establishes a baseline; movement while paused is excluded when you resume. This is not an all-day pedometer and does not use GPS.

Windows and phones without an available/permitted sensor use manual step entry. Enter the steps you actually walked, then save. Do not treat manually entered counts as sensor measurements.

14. Rating, developer & support

In Settings → About & support, open Rate Friction Atlas for the animated companion prompt. Rate now opens the platform’s real store listing when it is available. Skip delays another automatic request for seven days. “Don’t show this until a new version” saves that choice for the installed version/build.

Automatic requests wait for at least three days of use and three elapsed days, and only appear on an eligible Today screen after 45 seconds. They do not interrupt focus, a walk, a break, another screen or the tiny Windows companion. After three requests in one version, they stop until an update. Store listings are not ready yet, so automatic requests currently remain off.

About the developer shows Baishalya Roul, the installed app version, baisalya.com, help and the optional Buy me a coffee link. An external link opens your browser; coffee support does not grant app entitlements. A store opening is not proof that a review was submitted.

16. Troubleshooting

Digital data is empty

Confirm the Digital Friction master switch is on and Android Usage Access is granted. Usage data can take time to become useful if you just enabled it.

Short-form count does not move

Confirm Enhanced Tracking is enabled and its Accessibility Service is active. Counts remain estimates and supported app UI changes can affect detection.

Bot is not visible

On Android, confirm Display over other apps is allowed and the bot is started. On Windows, reopen the Mini Friction Bot from the app.

Correlation says “Building evidence”

Keep logging the habit. The engine intentionally waits for enough Done and Missed observations instead of inventing conclusions from a few days.
