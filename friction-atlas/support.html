Support

Fix the common things first.

Friction Atlas is local-first, so most problems can be diagnosed from device permissions, app settings and the feature you are trying to use.

Android Digital Friction

  • Confirm the master monitor is ON.
  • Confirm Android Usage Access is granted.
  • For short-form estimates, verify Enhanced Tracking is separately enabled.
  • Do not expect historical short-form counts before Enhanced Tracking existed.

Android Friction Bot

  • Allow Display over other apps.
  • Start the bot from Friction Atlas.
  • If paused/hidden, resume it from the Digital screen.
  • Turn the monitor off if you want all monitoring stopped.

Windows Mini Bot

  • Launch Mini Friction Bot from the app.
  • Drag it to reposition; its last position is remembered.
  • Use Open App to return to the full window.
  • Windows does not expose the same Android Usage Access data.

Correlation

  • Keep logging both Done and Blocked days.
  • At least 7 matched days are needed for an early signal.
  • You also need at least 3 Done and 3 Missed observations.
  • A weak relationship may correctly show “no clear association yet.”
FAQ

Common questions

Does Friction Atlas need an account?

Core habits, routines and focus work without a Friction Atlas account. Optional store purchases use platform billing and receipt verification when enabled.

Does the app read my messages?

The Enhanced short-form feature is designed not to persist messages, passwords, screenshots, typed content or raw screen text. See the Privacy Policy for the full disclosure.

Why is my Reels/Shorts number not exact?

It uses settled, identifiable short-form player/content transitions and deduplication. Unrecognized views can be skipped, and platform UI changes can affect detection. These remain estimates, not official Instagram/YouTube analytics.

Does Pro unlock every bot?

Future Pro Lifetime includes all catalog bots. Monthly and yearly Pro do not include paid bots. Individual bot purchases unlock only the selected bot. The current release does not require Pro for app features.

Why is an app not blocked during focus?

Select it in Focus guard before starting and leave the session guard switch on. Android needs the separate Focus guard Accessibility service. Windows needs the selected app open when refreshing the list, and Friction Atlas must keep running. Pause, breaks and the one-minute allowance intentionally release the guard.

Why does walking show zero or manual steps?

Android needs an available step sensor and optional physical activity access. The first counter reading is the session baseline; move with the phone after starting. Windows uses manual entries. Paused movement does not add to the walk.

Why is a visit’s item count a dash?

Per-visit recording begins with the update. A dash means it was not available for that visit; it does not mean you watched zero items. Earlier daily estimates stay in the daily total. Native OS session history and supported player layouts can be incomplete.

How do I stop rating reminders?

Choose Skip for a seven-day delay, or “Don’t show this until a new version” to suppress the installed version/build. You can still open Rate Friction Atlas manually from Settings. Automatic prompts remain off until the platform store listing is available.

Does coffee support unlock Pro?

No. It is optional external support for Baishalya Roul. Pro and paid bots are separate platform-store products.

Where can I get more help?

Email Baishalya Roul at baishalya1999@gmail.com. Include your app version, Android or Windows version and a short description. Review diagnostics or screenshots for private information before sending them. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or full habit backups.
