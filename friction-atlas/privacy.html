Privacy policy

Private by default. Optional by choice.

This policy is written for the Friction Atlas mobile/desktop app and its public website. It explains how the app handles local data, optional permissions and user-initiated sharing.

Effective: 2 November 2026·Developer: Baishalya Roul

1. Summary

Friction Atlas is a local-first habit, routine and focus application. Core use does not require a Friction Atlas account. Habit logs, planner records, settings and optional device-derived summaries stay on your device unless you choose to export/share them. External links, app-store reviews and optional purchase verification involve the relevant external service.

Habit, routine, focus and walking records are not sent as part of purchase verification. Friction Atlas does not sell habit data or use it to build advertising profiles.

2. Data stored locally by the app

Depending on which features you use, the app may store the following on your device:

  • habit names, schedules and daily Done/Blocked outcomes;
  • friction reasons, energy scores, notes and countermeasures;
  • experiment history and intervention outcomes;
  • weekly routines, dated occurrences, focus/break/walk sessions, selected guard apps and distraction counts;
  • session-relative sensor or manually entered step totals;
  • local first-install agreement record: accepted document revisions, UTC acceptance date and installed app version/build. Agreeing does not grant optional device permissions;
  • Android app visits reconstructed from OS foreground events and separate observed-view timestamps (package, time and kind only). These view timestamps use a rolling 30-day cleanup on feature use and a 50,000-event limit;
  • app settings, selected companion and local entitlement cache;
  • local rating-prompt preferences: usage-day count, first-use date, reminder delay and version/build suppression. These do not record the stars or content of a store review;
  • optional daily Digital Friction summaries such as foreground time, estimated app opens and observed short-form counts;
  • correlation evidence created from those local summaries and habit outcomes.

No custom Friction Atlas server is required for this core data.

Two different Accessibility services

Enhanced tracking examines limited supported player markers and transient content fingerprints to avoid counting every scroll. Raw view text, messages, passwords, typed content, screenshots and accessibility trees are not persisted by that detector. Counts remain estimates and third-party interfaces can change.

Focus guard is separately enabled after its disclosure. Screen-content retrieval is disabled for this service: it uses foreground app package changes, selected packages and the session deadline. Its overlay offers Back, a one-minute allowance and End. Pause, breaks and expiry release it. Essential system/call controls are excluded.

Android app visits

Visits use package/activity foreground and pause, screen-lock and shutdown events to group internal activity changes and close sessions. The view-event store does not persist captions, authors, screen text, screenshots or video identity fingerprints. Per-visit item counts are unavailable before that recording existed; old daily estimates are not redistributed across visits.

Windows app selection

Windows Focus guard identifies ordinary visible applications by executable path. Selected paths and session/distraction records remain local. It covers the selected application while Friction Atlas runs; it does not terminate that application or claim Android usage telemetry.

Your choice and permission controls

These permissions are optional and are requested for the feature you choose. You can stop monitoring or the companion in the app, end or pause a focus session, and revoke Usage Access, either Accessibility service, overlay access or physical activity permission in Android Settings. Revoking access stops the features that depend on it; core habit logging remains available. Walking uses a step sensor when available, or manual entry, rather than GPS location.

4. Purchases and app stores

When Store purchases are enabled, Google Play or Microsoft Store handles payments for Pro subscriptions, Pro Lifetime and separate bot products. Friction Atlas does not receive your card information.

The configured receipt-verification service may receive the app package name, product ID, purchase token and optional purchase ID to check validity and subscription expiry. The app keeps a bounded verified entitlement cache in secure local storage. It does not send habit, routine, walking or focus history with this request. Pro sales remain disabled for the current release. Bot purchase/restore is enabled only in eligible platform-store builds with configured products and receipt verification.

Store providers and verification hosting may process ordinary network metadata under their own policies. Store account records are separate from local app data; cross-platform ownership transfer is not promised.

5. Website

This static website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or embed third-party analytics in the supplied build. If hosted through GitHub Pages or another hosting provider, that provider may process standard network/request information such as IP address, user agent and request logs under its own terms.

External developer, store-review and Buy me a coffee links open services with their own privacy practices. No coffee widget or payment form is embedded in this product site. The app does not read submitted review text, star ratings or coffee payment details.

6. Retention, backup and deletion

Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears app storage, uninstalls the app, or imports/replaces data through supported backup features. “Delete Everything” is designed to remove local app data, including routines, focus/break/walk sessions, saved Digital Friction evidence and the separate observed-view timestamp records. App preferences such as rating-reminder choices and the local agreement record can remain until app storage is cleared.

Backup exports may contain private notes, habit history, routines, focus sessions, walking records and digital-activity summaries. Review exports before sharing them and store them in a location you trust. Exported backup files are controlled by the user and can remain wherever the user saves or shares them. Store purchase records are controlled by the relevant store and are not erased by deleting local habit data.

7. Security

Friction Atlas uses local storage and platform security capabilities appropriate to the app, including secure storage for selected sensitive local settings/entitlement cache and optional device/biometric authentication where available. No system can guarantee absolute security; users should protect device access and exported backup files.

8. Children

Friction Atlas is a general productivity/habit application and is not specifically directed to children. If the product is later offered to children in a regulated jurisdiction, the developer should update this policy and store disclosures before that distribution.
