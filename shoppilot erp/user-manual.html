05

Owner reporting

Read the business without accounting jargon

Open Reports from the Windows sidebar or More on a smaller device. Staff see only the branches, figures, and cost information allowed by their role.

Net Sales

Billed sales before tax minus refunds before tax for the selected period.

Cash Received

Money actually collected during the selected period. It can differ from billed sales.

Gross Profit

Net sales minus known cost of goods sold. Missing purchase cost can make this incomplete.

Expenses

Operating expenses recorded for the selected period and visible branch scope.

Operating Result

Gross profit minus operating expenses. The app can show an estimated result when costs are incomplete.

Customer Dues

Amounts customers still owe from saved bills and work records.

Report choiceWhat is available
DateToday, Yesterday, This week, This month, This year, Last year, or custom dates.
SummaryPrimary owner figures, previous-period comparison, management result, and trend.
Detail tabsBranches, Products, Stock, Customers, Suppliers, Staff, Cash, Expenses, and Tax.
ExportCSV export follows the same date, branch, and permission rules as the screen.

How to review a period

  1. Confirm the active workspace and branch scope.
  2. Choose a date preset or custom range.
  3. Read Net Sales and Cash Received separately.
  4. Check cost completeness before trusting profit.
  5. Open a detail tab to investigate the number.
  6. Export only when the recipient is authorised.

How periods are grouped

  • Today and Yesterday: one day.
  • This week and shorter custom ranges: daily trend.
  • This month: daily trend.
  • Medium custom ranges: Monday-based weekly trend.
  • This year and Last year: 12 monthly buckets, including empty months.
  • Long custom ranges: monthly trend.
!
Cost completeness matters

If an item has no reliable purchase cost, Gross Profit and Operating Result can be incomplete or estimated. Users without purchase-cost permission do not see restricted cost values, including in CSV exports.

Management report, not statutory accounts

Use these figures to run the shop and investigate operations. They do not replace statutory books, tax filing, audit, or advice from a qualified accountant.

06

Keep data in the right place

Workspaces, branches, owners, and staff

Workspace / Shop

A separate business database, such as “Main Repair Shop”.

Branch

A location inside the business, such as “City Branch”.

Owner

Controls the workspace, branches, staff, recovery, and most settings.

Staff

Works only in assigned branches and permitted modules.

Owner: how to add staff
  1. Sign in with the owner Google account.
  2. Open Business Tools → Staff (on a smaller device, open More first).
  3. Enter the staff member’s correct Google email.
  4. Select the correct branch.
  5. Choose only the permissions needed for that role.
  6. Keep the owner device online until sharing completes.
Staff: what access states mean
ActiveWork normally within the assigned branch and permissions.
SuspendedNew changes can be blocked. Contact the owner.
Removal in progressKeep the device online so already-authorized changes can finish delivery.
RemovedThe workspace is no longer available to that account.
07

Protect business data

Offline use, cloud backup, and recovery

1Local save

Normal allowed work is saved on the current device first, so the shop can continue without internet.

→
2Cloud delivery

When internet and authentication are available, waiting changes and backups are sent safely.

→
3New-device restore

The Google account finds the backup; the recovery code decrypts it.

🔑

Recovery key in one sentence

The recovery key unlocks your encrypted cloud backup on a replacement device after the original device is lost or damaged.

Set up backup safely

  1. Open Settings → Cloud backup → Backup.
  2. Connect the permanent owner Google account.
  3. Generate and confirm the recovery code.
  4. Export/save it outside this device.
  5. Run backup and wait for a safe result.

Restore after device loss

  1. Install ShopPilot on the replacement device.
  2. Sign in with the same authorized Google account.
  3. Select the discovered workspace.
  4. Choose Restore Data.
  5. Enter/import the matching recovery code.
  6. Check customers, recent work, invoices, stock, and branch.
Never do this while “changes waiting” is visible:
  • Do not uninstall ShopPilot.
  • Do not clear app data or storage.
  • Do not delete database or cloud files manually.
  • Do not sign out or change Google account before the app says it is safe.

ShopPilot is currently in private preview while real-device backup and restore qualification is completed. Follow the release and safety messages shown by the installed build.

09

When something looks wrong

Friendly problem guide

“Changes waiting”

Your work is saved on this device but has not finished cloud/team delivery. Connect internet, open Backup & Team Delivery, and keep the app open until safe.

“Secure cloud sign-in is required”

Reconnect the correct Google account from Cloud Backup. Do not reinstall the app.

“Recovery setup incomplete”

Open Settings → Cloud backup → Backup, confirm the recovery code, and save a separate copy.

“Conflict needs review”

The same information changed on more than one device. Open Review or Backup & Team Delivery, compare both versions, and choose carefully.

“Staff access suspended or removed”

Stop entering new work and contact the owner. The owner should review Staff and use Fix Access when offered.

Blank or loading screen

Wait briefly, check internet, reopen once, and follow the shown recovery/account action. Do not clear app data.

Use this order:
  1. Stop if the wrong workspace or branch is active.
  2. Read the friendly message and tap its action.
  3. Check internet and Google account.
  4. Open Backup & Team Delivery.
  5. Reopen the app once if needed.
  6. Export privacy-safe support diagnostics if the issue continues.
