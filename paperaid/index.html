Straight answers

Before you install PaperAid.

Does PaperAid upload my documents?

PaperAid's included document and photo tools are designed for local processing. Small release and business-configuration requests do not include document or photo bytes.

Is the first release paid?

No. The compiled launch fallback is free, with advertising, subscription prompts and premium locks disabled.

Are passport photos guaranteed to be accepted?

No. PaperAid is a preparation aid. Photo rules can change and some authorities require professional capture or additional certification. Always verify the exact current requirements of the authority receiving the photo.

Will ads appear on this website?

This product page contains one restrained consent-gated ad placement that stays hidden unless the shared baisalya.com monetization configuration is deliberately enabled and ready.
