Reuse

Question Bank

Store master questions with subject, chapter, difficulty, tags and favorites. Insert independent copies into papers so editing the paper does not silently change your saved master content.

Geometry Studio

Use lines, arrows, triangles, right triangles, square, rectangle, parallelogram, trapezium, rhombus, pentagon, hexagon, editable polygon, circle, semicircle, coordinate axes, number line, cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone and sphere.

OCR Question Capture

Choose Camera or Gallery, crop the relevant question and recognize English or Hindi text. The recognized text becomes editable content before you use it.

Professional Paper Styles + PDF/Word

Use school formal, compact, board-style, semester, institutional, minimal, coaching and primary-friendly layouts. Preview the paper and export to PDF or editable Word with real math typesetting; the certified path keeps structured formula source available instead of silently dropping unsupported content.

OMR Generator

Configure school/exam, number of questions, 2–5 options, roll field, section field and optional QR/barcode data, then generate an OMR PDF.

Document Reader & Converter

Rich in-app support for PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, PPTX and TXT, plus conversion workflows including DOCX→PDF, PDF→Word and TXT→Word. Older legacy Office formats are opened through compatible external apps rather than falsely pretending full rendering.

Scientific Calculator

Scientific functions, degree/radian mode, shift functions, hyperbolic mode, live result preview, previous answer, history and a built-in science formula catalog.
