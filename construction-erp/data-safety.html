Data Safety Overview

A plain-language summary of the inspected app behavior. The publisher must still complete Google Play Data safety and Microsoft Store declarations based on the exact production configuration.

Not a substitute for store forms: Firebase settings, sign-in methods, support processes and future SDKs can change the correct answers. Re-check the final release binary and console configuration.

Production checklist

  • Confirm the store developer/publisher name matches the Privacy Policy.
  • Publish working HTTPS privacy, support and account-deletion URLs.
  • Review Firebase Authentication, Firestore, App Check and Crashlytics settings.
  • Verify whether production support emails or logs create additional retained data.
  • Keep Data safety answers consistent with the final binary and actual business process.
  • Update this page whenever a new SDK, analytics tool, cloud attachment service or payment system is added.
