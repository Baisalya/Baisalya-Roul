BrightQuest Kids Support

Quick help for installation, parent controls, audio, progress and privacy.

01

App help

If BrightQuest is not behaving as expected, restart the app first. For a reproducible issue, include the device platform, app version, what you tapped, and what happened.

02

Parent lock

Use the recovery-code option on the parent gate if the PIN is forgotten. Keep the recovery code outside the app and never send it to support.

03

Audio & narration

Parents can control master audio, background music, game sounds and narrator settings separately from the Parent Center.

04

Privacy

The current app stores learner profiles and progress locally. Read the full policy before store installation or contacting support.

Need human help?

Contact the developer.

For BrightQuest questions, use email and include enough detail to reproduce the problem. Do not send a child’s full legal name, parent PIN, recovery code or payment details.
