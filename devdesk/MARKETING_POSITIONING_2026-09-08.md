# DevDesk marketing positioning — 2026-09-08

## Core position

**DevDesk is one local-first workspace for learning, research, organization, creation, software building, and technical operations.**

The product should not be marketed as developer-only. Developer depth is a strength, not the entry requirement.

## Audience messages

- **Students:** turn notes and material into structured study work; plan assignments and connect knowledge.
- **Researchers:** organize source notes, relationships, diagrams and project knowledge.
- **Professionals and non-developers:** plan work, organize files/notes, draft, use visual views and AI-assisted workflows without needing code.
- **Developers:** keep APIs, Git, Diff, agents, terminals and project context together.
- **Technical operators/builders:** manage Docker and Compose applications, create local Kubernetes clusters, deploy and troubleshoot applications, inspect raw resources, and use compatibility/safety controls from the same project-aware workspace.

## Marketing guardrails

- Never imply every feature is free. Preserve Builder/All Access monetization and defer price claims to current store/app configuration.
- Never imply Android runs a local Docker or Kubernetes runtime.
- Do not claim remote container management in this release.
- Keep local-first, review-first, deterministic troubleshooting, compatibility-as-data, RBAC/context identity checks, and explicit destructive-action safety visible.
- Market real workflows and outcomes, not invented AI autonomy.
