Privacy

Website privacy

How baisalya.com handles contact requests, support links and optional advertising.

Contact forms

When you submit a contact form, the information you enter is sent to the configured form delivery provider so Baishalya Roul can respond. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or other secrets.

Product websites

Product pages may link to stores, documentation, GitHub, support services and Buy Me a Coffee. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled by default in this website source. If manual advertising is enabled later, a third-party advertising provider such as Google may process browser or device information under its own policies and any consent requirements that apply. The site is designed to use restrained manual placements rather than automatic ad insertion.

Support and payments

Buy Me a Coffee and product-store purchases are handled by the respective third-party services. baisalya.com does not process card details directly.

Contact

For privacy questions email baishalya1999@gmail.com.
